Generado con la herramienta integrada imagegen para este proyecto. Es una imagen ficticia; no representa la finca de una persona ni una ubicación verificada. Se utiliza como fondo ilustrativo en Dashboard, Campos, Mapas, Riego, Fertilización y Siembra en modo demo. Los polígonos, capas y etiquetas se dibujan en SVG y los controles de mapa son componentes de la interfaz. En modo conectado, Leaflet muestra las geometrías registradas sobre las teselas del proveedor configurado. Mapas en AgroVision

Prompt utilizado:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: background raster for an agricultural dashboard interactive map. Create a realistic, beautiful strictly top-down drone aerial photograph of a tropical Colombian banana plantation, landscape 3:2 composition. A large agricultural estate with five clear adjacent irregular rectangular green banana plantation blocks occupying most of the image, many precise parallel rows of banana trees, separated by narrow beige dirt tracks. Dense lush tropical forest surrounds the estate at all edges, a winding blue-green river along the far right edge, a tiny simple farm shed in the lower right. Rich natural forest greens, sunlit lime banana leaves, subtle warm tropical sunlight, high-resolution fine canopy texture, professional satellite agricultural imagery, natural photography. No text, no labels, no borders, no UI, no colored heatmaps, no diagrams, no watermarks. This will be a fictional illustrative demo basemap, not real geospatial data.

Fotografía de campo de Fertilización

La imagen fertilization-fieldwork.jpg se generó con ImageGen y acompaña las vistas de registros cuando la selección está vacía. La escena es ilustrativa.

photorealistic-natural. Create a wide landscape editorial photograph for AgroVision’s fertilization workspace in a tropical Colombian banana farm. A close-up of dark, crumbly soil at the base of a healthy young banana plant, a field technician’s earth-stained work glove releasing a small pinch of pale granular fertilizer next to it, mature rows and broad green leaves fading softly behind. Natural warm early-morning light, vivid botanical greens and cocoa-brown soil, practical farm work, high-end documentary photography, precise realistic textures. Compose the plant and the visible action centrally and toward the right so the photograph can be cropped to a wide 2:1 horizontal panel. Landscape 3:2 image. No text, graphics, logos, diagrams, labels or artificial overlays.
